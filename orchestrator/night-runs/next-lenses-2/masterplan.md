## 1. Executive Strategic Thesis

**Maculis wins on trustworthy surprise, not on integrations.** The organising law
this epic discovered is the **evidence-availability axis** (WS-1): every candidate
lens is Tier A (the subject's own public surface), Tier B (the wider public web),
Tier C (data the entrepreneur volunteers), or Tier D (genuinely unobservable). The
strategic consequences are sharp and, in places, the opposite of the obvious
hypothesis:

1. **The most valuable lenses cover no classic business domain at all.** The
   highest-ranked, most magical lenses are cheap Tier-A signals about how a business
   presents itself (What You Repeat, Price Visibility, Proposition Backing). The
   classic domains everyone expects — Finance, Cashflow, Margin, Market, Innovation,
   Commercial Performance — are **Tier D and were killed** as public lenses: you
   cannot ground them on evidence, so they would make Maculis a confidently-wrong
   guesser and destroy the trust that is the whole moat.
2. **Zero-integration is the strategy, not a fallback.** Maculis must deliver a real,
   evidence-bound "I never saw that" moment within minutes, with no account and no
   coupling. Integrations are earned later, by insight, one at a time — never a wall
   the entrepreneur must climb before value appears (WS-6).
3. **The portfolio is a funnel to GrowBrain.** Each lens discovers something checkable;
   the discovery creates honest curiosity; GrowBrain answers "why is this happening and
   what do I do about it." The lenses are free/earned; GrowBrain is the depth product
   (WS-8, WS-9).
4. **The relationship compounds without pooling PII.** Lens signals are evidence with
   provenance, human-gated, consent-fail-closed; the flywheel gets smarter each
   interaction while staying on the friend side of the friend-or-stalker line (WS-8).

**The arc:** First Five (one magical lens) → a growing catalogue of Tier-A surprise
lenses → optional owner-consented enrichment → GrowBrain → Pass the Lens sharing that
feeds the top of the funnel again.

## 2. The decision that matters most — Lens #2

> ## Lens #2 = **"What You Repeat"** (N-REPEAT)
> **What we build:** a Tier-A lens that computes term/­phrase frequency over the
> subject's own public copy and surfaces the word or idea the business is
> unconsciously obsessed with.
> **Why this one:** it wins the four-factor Lens-#2 test (feasibility × WOW ×
> low-data × GrowBrain pull). It is close to trivial and *trivially reliable* —
> arithmetic on text the entrepreneur can re-count — so it satisfies
> precision-over-recall by construction and fails closed on thin text. It delivers a
> visceral, honest "I never saw that" moment and its depth question writes itself.
> **What it shows:** "You say *[word]* more than anything else. Here is the shape of
> what your site is really about." One grounded sentence, no number shown, no score.
> **Data needed:** none beyond the public page already fetched. Tier A, zero PII,
> zero accounts, zero external dependency.
> **Value with zero integration:** full — it *is* a zero-integration lens; minutes to
> first wow.
> **Couplings that strengthen it later:** none required. Optionally, Google Search
> Console (owner-consented, WS-4/WS-5 Build-Now) can later add the *other* half — what
> customers actually search — turning the question into a grounded comparison. That is
> an upgrade, never a prerequisite.
> **The magic (WS-7):** the reveal reframes the founder's relationship to their own
> voice in one line; highly shareable (Pass the Lens).
> **GrowBrain bridge (WS-8):** "You keep saying *[word]*. Do you want to understand
> why, and what your customers are actually looking for?" — a natural doorway, not an
> upsell; consent + provenance carried in the `lens_signal` handoff.

**Red-team refinement (mine, over WS-1).** WS-1 and WS-7 are both right and I am
tightening the claim: the lens can *reliably* show the repetition pattern (Tier A,
arithmetic), but it **cannot assert** "your customer would use a different word" —
that needs customer-side evidence (Tier C/D). So the honest Lens #2 **reveals the
pattern and poses the mismatch as the GrowBrain question**, never asserts it. This
keeps it on the right side of the "great demo vs. durable, checkable insight" line
that WS-7 flagged.

**Immediate commercial follow-on:** **Price Visibility & Confidence** (D-PRICE) —
ranked #1 overall and the commercial lead, shipped in Wave B *after* the negative-
control harness is battle-tested, because it carries a real reliability landmine
(hiding price is correct for many B2B/service models, so it must fail closed rather
than scold). **Pass-the-Lens alternative:** The Echo (N-ECHO).

## 4. First Five Enhancement Plan (WS-2)

Deepen without lengthening or diluting the magic — **depth is bought by sharpening
existing sentences, not adding them** (default verdict on a new signal: reject unless
it displaces a weaker line). Spend the "see more" budget on **invisible intelligence**
(render-as-seen, multi-witness gating, verify-fast evidence ranking, phrase
compression — nothing new on screen). Only four new binary, already-fetched-surface
signals proposed (template tab title; page-talks-only-about-itself; dead primary
button; promise-vs-ask), each precision-gated and replace-not-append. Reworked
synthesis (single-thread), recognition-ordered reveal, self-referential comparison
(change vs the subject's own past snapshot — never a benchmark), an honest quiet
Meaningful End, limit-becomes-question follow-ups, evidence-only relationship memory,
four new next-lens triggers. Explicit kill-list: reading-level/tone/ratio/benchmark/
NPS/any score = noise.

## 5. Finance — the honest reconciliation (WS-1 kill vs WS-3 deep dive vs WS-7 rename)

Three workstreams collided on "finance"; the resolution is a clean three-way split:

| Thing | Tier | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public "Finance/Cashflow/Margin" lens** (read the books from the web) | D | **KILLED** (WS-1). Ungroundable → confidently wrong. Never ship. |
| **"Timing Gap / true runway"** from 4–6 founder-typed numbers incl. the forgotten quarterly BTW hit (WS-3) | C | **Viable, but not a First Five lens.** An honest self-estimate tool (labelled as the founder's own numbers, never dressed as analysis). Candidate GrowBrain/Relationship-Workspace utility, not a zero-account First Five surprise. |
| **"Price Visibility & Confidence"** — how price is *presented* on the public site (WS-7's proposed rename of the overclaiming "Finance Lens") | A | **Ship in Wave B** as the commercial lead. This is the real, groundable "finance-adjacent" lens. |

On integrations, WS-3 is decisive: **"connect all the banks" is the wrong first
move** — PSD2 is licence-encumbered, consent-expiring (90-day, 20–40% attrition) and
rate-limited (~4 calls/day) → a *decaying* feed. If a connected Finance tool is ever
built, the order is **payments/commerce first** (Mollie/Stripe — timely, founder-
owned), then a CSV import that captures ~80% of the value, and PSD2 last/never.

## 6. Marketing (WS-4) & the Integration Opportunity Matrix (WS-5)

- **Marketing Lens:** zero-integration first, answering one alignment question — "does
  the public evidence match what you are trying to do?" Adopt **exactly one** coupling:
  **Google Search Console** (read-only, first-party, owner-consented, no visitor PII;
  reveals the query-vs-intent gap). **Kill** Meta Marketing / Google Ads / HubSpot for
  v1; **reject** competitor ad-transparency "spying" as noisy and creepy.
- **Integration matrix (27 rows, 26 official sources):** only **3 Build Now** —
  **KVK Handelsregister** (free, public, the identity spine feeding every lens),
  **VIES VAT validation** (free EU service), **Google Search Console** (free, consented).
  **Avoid:** LinkedIn Marketing API. Everything else is Research-Next/Defer, including
  the tempting free open-banking tier — because the Finance path is secure import, not
  a live API.

## 7. Zero-Integration Strategy (WS-6)

Three permitted rung-0 inputs only: **public data + 1–3 earned questions (asked only
after an observation) + one optional one-time safe upload.** A four-rung ladder per
lens — **zero → optional enrichment → connected → longitudinal** — with the next step
pulled by insight, never gated by a data wall. Cross-lens rule: the public read runs
first and silently; one evidence-bound observation lands *before* any question or data
ask. Nine anti-patterns rejected (the data wall, empty-state dashboard, score/verdict,
fake precision, consent laundering). Honest flags: the Finance rung-0 is thin (skip
straight to connected commerce); Customer/CX depends on public review volume.

## 8. WOW/Magic (WS-7) & the experience per top lens

For each top lens WS-7 specifies the arc — *Sees → Expects → Discovers → the "hè?" →
Reveal → Remembers → Shows*. No dashboards; one grounded qualitative sentence per
reveal, never a number, no-dash copy. Durability ranking (in words, not scores):
Findability strongest (the shareable artefact *is* the share); Price Visibility and
The Dead End strong (external, un-arguable evidence); First Five endures as a reusable
reframe. Honest caution: any lens that is a great demo but has no "remember tomorrow"
value (the interpretive "Squint") must be pinned to a fail-closable claim or kept only
as the presentation metaphor.

## 9. GrowBrain bridge & Relationship Flywheel (WS-8)

Per-lens GrowBrain bridge = `propose_growbrain` action landing in `ai_draft` for a
human (never auto-advance): trigger → honest transition → handed-over context → one
concrete first step → the exact `lens_signal` fields carried → consent/provenance
preserved. The **8-hop flywheel** (lens → insight → conversation → memory → next lens
→ GrowBrain → new insight → Pass the Lens) is grounded hop-by-hop in the real
copilot → `ai_draft` → human machinery, and gets smarter without pooling PII.
**Creepiness guardrails** are explicit: a friend-or-stalker test per hop, a hard
analysis-vs-outreach bright line, and a reject-list for provenance-less/consent-less/
cross-tenant/enrichment handoffs. Critical flags: Finance is the highest
upsell/manipulation risk ("no real free evidence-cited value at the point of curiosity
→ no bridge"); external Reputation drifts into third-party surveillance and is rejected
as a silent handoff.

## 10. Data / privacy / provenance architecture (WS-9 + EPIC-1)

Make **"lens" a thin platform primitive now, not a speculative framework.** A
declarative **lens contract** (observes / data_requirements / evidence / confidence /
freshness / consent / privacy_posture / reveal / meaningful_end / next_lens_triggers /
growbrain_handoff / evaluation / longitudinal) and one **signal contract** — a single
tenant-scoped, PII-free `lens_signal` table behind the existing `ai_draft` suggestion.
Longitudinal comparison = two `lens_signal` rows over time. Inherits tenant isolation,
consent-fail-closed, provenance and retention/erasure from the real schema. **Do not**
build a lens marketplace / dynamic runtime / rules-DSL until the portfolio proves
demand.

## 13. Concrete Phase-0 implementation proposal

Smallest shippable slice that proves the strategy (no speculative framework):

1. **Lens contract v0** — a declarative manifest per the WS-9 field list; add a `lens`
   label to the existing `ai_draft` surface; add the single `lens_signal` table
   (tenant-scoped, PII-free, `evidence[] + source + model + freshness_at`).
2. **Lens #2 "What You Repeat"** as the first lens on the contract: term-frequency over
   the subject's own fetched copy; fail-closed on thin text; two-bucket strength; a
   one-sentence reveal that shows the pattern and **poses** (never asserts) the
   customer-mismatch as the GrowBrain question; CI negative-controls proving it stays
   silent without evidence.
3. **KVK + VIES identity spine** behind the subject/relationship model (free, public) —
   infrastructure that every later lens reuses.
4. **Nothing else.** No new product features beyond this until Lens #2 is validated in
   the pilot (per the assignment: decision-quality first, not code-volume).

Phase-0 code work is autonomous once repo access to `maculis-first-five.` is granted
(HUMAN ACTION); the design, contracts and this masterplan are complete now.

## 14. Red-team summary (what we killed / tightened)

- **Killed** as ungroundable public lenses: Finance, Cashflow, Margin, Market,
  Innovation, Commercial Performance, external Competitor-ID, Branded Search.
- **Refused** the "connect everything" reflex: only 3 integrations are Build-Now;
  PSD2 open banking is discouraged (decaying, licence-heavy); most APIs are Defer.
- **Tightened Lens #2**: reveal the repetition pattern (reliable), pose the customer
  mismatch as a question (do not assert beyond Tier-A evidence).
- **Separated** the three "finance" things (killed public lens / Tier-C runway tool /
  Tier-A price-presentation lens) so none overclaims.
- **Flagged creepy**: Founder-as-person, external Reputation surveillance, competitor
  spying, enrichment — rejected or hard-gated.
- **Reconciled** the numeric-reveal tension (WS-7 vs doc-01 examples): internal
  arithmetic is fine; the *public* reveal is always one qualitative sentence, never a
  number or score.
- **Provenance honesty:** WS-3/4/5 note the egress proxy blocked full-page fetches, so
  exact pricing/quota figures rest on official-domain search snippets and are flagged
  for human confirmation before any commercial commitment.
