# Competitor and Adjacent-Product Landscape for Maculis (business lenses)

> Research artifact for the Lens Strategy Masterplan. Compiled 2026-08-15, current to 2025-2026.
> Scope: financial/advisory tools, cross-function business-health products, AI-native SMB
> co-pilots, and the Dutch/EU SMB context. Facts are sourced; interpretation is labelled. Several
> product-comparison pages are vendor blogs with self-interested framing, treated cautiously.

---

## Category 1: Financial insight / cashflow / advisory tools (SMB + accountants)

- **Fathom.** Reporting, three-way forecasting (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow), consolidation, KPI
  tracking, board packs. Deep Xero/QuickBooks integration. Interpretation: a reporting and modelling
  engine, not an insight engine. It makes numbers legible and forecastable; a human interprets.
  Strong evidence layer, thin autonomous meaning.
- **Float.** Direct cash-flow forecasting, weekly granularity, Xero/QuickBooks/FreeAgent. Single
  function depth (cash), no cross-signal ambition.
- **Futrli (by Sage).** Short-term daily/weekly cash prediction, marketed as AI-driven. The AI is
  predictive time series on transactions, not cross-function reasoning.
- **Spotlight Reporting.** Multi-entity consolidation, forecasting, board reports, advisor-facing.
  Same class as Fathom: polished evidence, analyst-supplied meaning.
- **Agicap (FR, strong EU).** Best-in-class treasury data plumbing in Europe: real-time multi-bank
  cash position via Open Banking + ERP connectors, rolling 13-week forecasting, AP automation with
  OCR. Repositioning to mid-market CFOs in 2026. Insight is confined to liquidity: answers when do I
  run out of cash, not which part of the business is silently causing it.
- **Fluidly (DEFUNCT).** Intelligent cashflow startup, ~1,100 accounting partners, ~500,000
  connected businesses. Acquired by OakNorth Dec 2021, shut down April 2023, folded into the bank.
  Cautionary case: the most AI-cashflow-branded UK player of its era did not survive standalone;
  insight branding became a lending funnel.
- **Pulse.** Lightweight cash-flow tracking for small businesses/freelancers. No cross-function
  reach. Appears static.
- **Silverfin (BE/NL, now Visma).** Cloud post-accounting/compliance-and-reporting platform, central
  to accounting firms. Acquired by Visma (2024). ~850 direct customers (mostly firms), ~320,000 end
  clients. AI: chart-of-accounts standardisation (AI Mapping), anomaly detection, reconciliation, an
  AI Assistant, and in March 2026 launched "Talking" (surfacing advisory conversation opportunities
  out of compliance data; cited 40,000 advisory discussions from 400 clients). The most strategically
  relevant NL/BE incumbent for the advisory layer. But it is accountant-facing, compliance-anchored,
  and firm-workflow shaped, not owner-facing revelation.
- **Uplinq.** AI-assisted bookkeeping plus human experts. Insight is a byproduct, not the product.
- **Digits.** Autonomous General Ledger, AI-native accounting trained on $825B+ SMB transactions.
  Auto-categorises, reconciles, drafts financials; launched AI Agents for accounting (June 2025);
  dynamic dashboards. Real AI in the bookkeeping layer, but the surfaced output is still dynamic
  dashboards of the standard finance quartet. Intelligence spent on producing clean numbers, not on
  revealing cross-function tensions.
- **Puzzle.** AI-native accounting for venture-backed startups; real-time burn/runway. Niche,
  metric-centric, not cross-lens.
- **Runway (runway.com FP&A).** Collaborative FP&A; connects 700+ tools (ERP, HRIS, CRM, accounting)
  to auto-update actuals; AI Copilot for natural-language queries (Why did revenue drop in Q3) and
  narrative board summaries. The closest philosophical cousin to Maculis in this category. But it
  targets startup/scaleup finance teams, not SMB owners, and the why stays largely finance-internal
  (revenue drivers), not genuinely cross-domain. The strongest insight-as-narrative execution to
  study.
- **Basedash / Fintool.** Off-target: Basedash is internal-tools/BI over your database; Fintool is a
  financial-research copilot for institutional investors. Fintool is a useful pattern reference
  (source-cited natural-language answers over documents) but for capital markets.
- **Bilendo (DE).** AR/credit-management/receivables automation. Single function (collections/DSO),
  narrow.

## Category 2: Business-health / benchmarking / cross-function KPI products

- **Scoro.** Business-management platform combining KPI dashboards, project management, and financial
  reporting for professional-services firms. Genuinely spans projects + finance + utilisation, but it
  is an operations suite with dashboards. Cross-function data coexists; it is not cross-interpreted.
- **Databox, Grow.com, Klipfolio, Geckoboard.** KPI/BI dashboard tools. The archetype of data
  without meaning: they aggregate metrics from many sources into one screen. Connecting sources is not
  connecting signals: a marketing tile and a finance tile sit side by side, but the tool never says
  these two are in tension. Meaning is 100% offloaded to the viewer. Precisely the gap Maculis targets.
- **Bilbeo (acquired/inactive).** Self-described world's first auto-populated business analytics,
  using algorithms to auto-build dashboards, find correlations, pinpoint key influencers, flag KPIs,
  and give plain-language recommendations. Conceptually the closest historical attempt to surface
  surprising relationships automatically for SMBs. That it was acquired and faded is a signal: the
  idea is right but hard to make sticky and trustworthy. A key reference and a cautionary tale.
- **Baremetrics, ChartMogul.** Subscription-revenue analytics (MRR/ARR/churn/LTV, cohorts).
  Baremetrics' Cancellation Insights is a nice micro-example of connecting a why to a number, but
  stays inside revenue. Deep in one domain, not cross-function.
- **Cardata.** Vehicle/mileage reimbursement analytics. Off-target, excluded.

Category verdict: no one in the mainstream business-health tier genuinely cross-interprets functions.
Scoro co-locates them; dashboards merely co-display them; Bilbeo tried true correlation-mining and did
not durably win. The white space is real.

## Category 3: AI-native business co-pilot / advisor for SMB owners (2024-2026)

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot Business.** Announced Ignite 2025, GA Dec 2026; for SMBs up to 300 users,
  ~$21/user/mo; enterprise Copilot inside Office apps; Wave 3 (early 2026) added Copilot Cowork with
  model choice including Claude. Horizontal productivity AI: it can answer about your data if pointed
  at it, but has no opinionated model of business health and no cross-lens reveals. A substrate and a
  competitor for attention, not a lens product.
- **Agentic CFO signals.** Mastercard's forthcoming Virtual C-Suite is cited as embedding agentic CFO
  capabilities into accounting platforms; tools like n8n let SMBs wire bank APIs to anomaly alerts.
  Direction-of-travel signal: agentic CFO is becoming a category, but most implementations are
  alerting/automation, not revelation. Expect the label to crowd; differentiate on quality of insight,
  not the AI CFO tagline.
- **AI-native accounting agents (Truewind, Rillet, Basis, Numeric, Pilot).** Enormous capital is going
  into making the books faster and cleaner (the evidence layer). Almost none into revelation across
  functions. Complements to Maculis, not competitors: they produce cleaner inputs Maculis could read.

Category verdict: AI co-pilot for SMB today is horizontal productivity (Microsoft), autonomous
bookkeeping/close (Truewind/Digits/Rillet), or natural-language-query over a finance model (Runway).
None is an owner-facing, cross-domain reveal engine. The AI CFO branding arrives fast, but the
substance is automation, not insight.

## Category 4: Dutch / EU SMB context

- **KVK.** Business Register, Figures and trends, and a Business Register Open Dataset (basic company
  data, daily updates). A genuine external source for concentration/sector context and benchmarking.
  Reference data, not insight.
- **CBS.** Staat van het MKB 2025, sector surveys, digitalisation/AI statistics. MKB firm count +3.1%
  in 2025 (driven by zzp'ers). SME AI adoption rising but low. Sector benchmarks Maculis could anchor
  "your ratio vs sector" lenses to; low adoption is both risk (education) and opportunity (greenfield).
- **Exact Online.** Dominant NL SMB accounting/ERP: 400,000+ SMEs; native insights are dashboards; the
  analytics is commonly offloaded to Power BI / BrightAnalytics. That offload is exactly where an
  insight layer plugs in. Integration with Exact is close to a prerequisite for a serious NL play.
- **Visma ecosystem (Silverfin, Nmbrs, eAccounting).** Visma is building the accountant-facing advisory
  stack. The gap they leave is owner-facing revelation.
- **Bizcuit, Yuki, Nmbrs, Informer.** A dense NL automation/plumbing layer feeding transactions,
  invoices, payroll. All aggregation and workflow; none interprets across functions. Rich integration
  surface for Maculis; none is a competitor at the insight layer.
- **Peppol / e-invoicing.** ~1.4M companies on Peppol; EU mandates rolling out 2025-2027; data
  analytics named an active innovation cluster. Mandated e-invoicing creates a standardised,
  machine-readable stream of who-sells-what-to-whom across the economy. A strategic tailwind: invoice-
  level data powers customer-concentration, pricing, and buyer/supplier-dependency lenses. Nobody has
  built owner-facing insight on top of it; it is treated as compliance plumbing.

## Synthesis: where the market actually is

1. Where dashboards dominate (data without meaning). The entire BI/KPI tier, the native insights of
   Exact/QuickBooks/Xero, and the output layer of the AI-bookkeeping players all stop at display. They
   connect data sources into one screen but never connect signals into a claim. Dashboard fatigue is a
   well-evidenced pain (Sigma, LogRocket, Fabi.ai): too many metrics, no signal/noise separation, no
   decision owner, action paralysis.
2. Who genuinely connects cross-function signals. Almost no one, for SMB owners. Runway connects
   many systems and wraps them in narrative, but for finance teams and within a finance model. Scoro
   co-locates. Bilbeo attempted automated correlation/root-cause and did not durably survive.
   Silverfin's Talking surfaces where advice is warranted from compliance data, the nearest incumbent
   motion toward revelation, but firm-facing. The cross-function-reveal space for owners is effectively
   empty.
3. Where AI is used well vs veneer. Used well: transaction-intent classification at scale (Digits),
   close automation (Truewind, Rillet), natural-language query over a finance model (Runway), document
   extraction/anomaly detection (Silverfin). These do real work on the evidence layer. Veneer: AI
   slapped on time-series cash prediction, actionable insights that are threshold alerts on single
   KPIs, AI CFO branding over alerting. The reliable tell: AI is real when it reduces the cost of
   producing clean numbers; it is veneer when it claims to produce meaning but only recolours a
   dashboard or fires a threshold alert.
4. The insight-vs-evidence gap (the central finding). The market has poured capital into the evidence
   layer (clean, real-time, reconciled, connected data) and almost nothing into the insight layer (what
   does this mean, across functions, that the owner cannot see). Everyone is racing to make numbers
   available and correct; interpretation is still left to a human advisor or to the owner staring at
   tiles. The gap is structural: automation ROI is measurable and easy to sell; revelation is hard to
   productise, hard to make trustworthy, and hard to benchmark. Bilbeo and Fluidly both show the idea is
   attractive but historically fragile.

## Where Maculis differentiation is real vs illusory

Real differentiation (defensible):
1. Cross-lens reveals as the product, not a feature. Revenue grows but cash does not, one-customer
   concentration rising, brand promises what customers do not experience: relationships between
   functions. No SMB-owner-facing product sells this. Runway is the only near-analog and it is finance-
   team, finance-model bound. Genuinely open space.
2. Owner-facing, not accountant-facing. Silverfin/Visma own the advisor layer; Exact/Bizcuit own
   plumbing. The entrepreneur's own interpretive layer is unclaimed, and the advisor-facing incumbents
   structurally will not pivot to owner psychology.
3. Surprise and "what you under-see" as the framing. Dashboards optimise for completeness; the
   documented failure mode is exactly that completeness. A selective, revelatory product (few,
   surprising, high-consequence findings) is the direct antidote to dashboard fatigue.
4. Riding NL/EU structured-data tailwinds. Exact integration + Peppol e-invoicing + KVK/CBS benchmarks
   make concentration, dependency, and vs-sector lenses feasible on standardised data before most
   markets. A timing and geography edge.

Illusory or at-risk differentiation:
1. AI CFO / AI advisor as a label. Already crowding (Microsoft, Mastercard Virtual C-Suite, every
   accounting-AI vendor). Branding on this alone is not a moat; the substance must be reveal quality.
2. "We connect all your data." Databox, Klipfolio, Runway, Digits all say this. Source-connection is
   table stakes. The differentiation is cross-interpretation, and messaging must not blur into another
   integrated dashboard.
3. "Actionable insights from your KPIs." Used by Bilbeo, Geckoboard, most BI tools for threshold
   alerts. If Maculis reveals degrade into KPI X crossed threshold Y, it collapses into the veneer tier.
4. Correlation-mining as the core engine. Bilbeo already tried auto-find-correlations and did not
   durably survive. Raw correlation-surfacing is a trap: it produces noise and false surprises and
   erodes trust. Defensibility is a curated, causally-plausible, explained set of lenses with evidence,
   not a correlation firehose. Trust and restraint are the moat, not cleverness.

One-line strategic read: the industry has largely finished (or is well-funded to finish) the evidence
layer and left the insight layer empty for SMB owners. Maculis is differentiated to the exact extent
that it resists becoming another dashboard or another AI CFO alerting tool, and instead delivers a
small number of trustworthy, cross-function reveals the owner could not have seen, explained with the
underlying evidence. The risk is not that the space is occupied; it is that insight is genuinely hard
to make trustworthy, and prior attempts (Bilbeo, Fluidly) proved the idea seductive but fragile.

This directly validates the frozen Reveal Engine's stance: strict gate, silence as success, no causal
claims, provenance on everything. That discipline is the antidote to exactly the failure modes that
sank the previous attempts.
