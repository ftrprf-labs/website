# Executable Lens Specification: Dependency and Resilience

> Implemented by `dependency.mjs`. Runs on the shared engine. The wildcard candidate and the purest
> cross-lens reveal: a business that looks healthy while several loose sources reveal one hidden
> vulnerability.

- Entrepreneur question: how much of my business quietly depends on a single customer, supplier, person,
  or on me, and how transferable is it.
- Available evidence: finance (surface health from two periods, customer concentration), relationship
  routing (share of active customer relationships where the owner is personally the contact, from
  Relationship Intelligence), supplier concentration, channel dependency. It both reads and writes the
  relationship picture, which is why its relationship-intelligence richness is the highest of the three.
- Observation types (`observe`): operational_fact (surface health, concentrations) and external_signal
  (channel). Confidence L3 to L4.
- Relation families (`relate`): the flagship is a cross-lens CONTRADICTION, "looks resilient, is fragile"
  (surface health versus one hidden concentration). A cross-lens CONCENTRATION for owner-in-the-middle
  under growth. A single-domain CONCENTRATION for a pure customer-concentration fact when the surface is
  not healthy.
- Candidate generation and wording: name the surface health and the one dependency in tension. Neutral,
  never alarmist, never causal.
- Confidence: L4 for the flagship (rests on hard finance plus routing), L3 for the routing relation.
- Freshness: slow-moving; recomputed when finance or relationship data updates.
- Provenance: finance file figures plus a derived relationship-routing figure. The derived figure must be
  labelled as derived, never presented as a hard fact.
- Gate: the frozen gate unchanged. The flagship passes because it is a cross-lens CONTRADICTION (fits the
  undeniable set and the cross-lens path). The single-domain CONCENTRATION is SUPPRESSED by the frozen
  gate, exactly like Finance's, which the engine self-test proves. So Dependency is engine-compatible only
  when its quantitative facts are framed as cross-lens contradictions; a naive single-domain build would
  be suppressed.
- Silence conditions: a diversified, healthy business with no dominant dependency, or a business whose
  surface is not healthy (the flagship needs the surprising gap between health and fragility). Measured
  silence rate 64 percent.
- Revealworthiness: the vulnerability must be material (a concentration at or above a stated threshold)
  and must genuinely contrast with a healthy surface, so the reveal is a surprise, not a scolding.
- Recognition: ja, voor een deel, nee. Highest offense risk of the three, so a nee that means "no, that
  is wrong" matters as much as a nee that means "I had not seen that"; the pilot must distinguish them.
- Meaningful End (candidate): "Je bedrijf is gezonder dan dit ene punt. Maar dit ene punt wilde ik je
  laten zien."
- Next-lens trigger: it is itself a terminal, integrative lens; it draws from Finance and Reputation
  rather than pointing onward.
- GrowBrain bridge: strong (reduce the dependency, make the business more transferable), and the
  experiments are meaningful, though slower to measure than Finance's.
- Empirical profile (measured): reveal rate 36 percent, mean WOW 5.0 (highest), mean non-triviality 5.0
  (highest), mean evidence strength 4.0, mean false-positive risk 2.0. Engine-compatible via cross-lens
  framing (zero suppressions in the case-set).
- Why Lens 4: it depends on both the Finance connector and Relationship Intelligence for its evidence,
  and it carries the highest offense risk, so it is built once the platform and the owner relationship are
  mature.
